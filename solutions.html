WOMANY

Women's Health Application

Womany is an application that serves as a health companion to women. It provides the foundation for a holistic approach over a life course to enable women and girls with access to best care practices, medical professionals, pathways for research, diagnosis, and treatment, to reduce suffering and improve quality of life.

Large Language Models

The use of Large Language Models (LLM's) within Womany serves to democratise access to specialised women's health knowledge and aid patients and clinicians in personalised best care practices and treatment decisions.

Beyond Language

The practice of diagnosis and prognosis is inherently multimodal, incorporating information from images, electronic health records, sensors, wearables, genomics and more. The LLM is extended to leverage this data at scale using self-supervised and reinforcement learning.

Metrics & Data

The life-critical nature of LLM's in health contexts necessitates establishing comprehensive metrics for evaluation. All data is managed with privacy preservation using differential privacy and federated learning.

WOMANYStudies

Research and Clinical Trials

The state-of-the-art research platform to enable the collection of good quality healthcare data, in a trusted and safe way for the management and tracking of chronic disorders and diseases. WomanyStudies aims to democratise exceptional care and the research process to accelerate effective diagnostics, treatments, and pathways cures in women's health.
